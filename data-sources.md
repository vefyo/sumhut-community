Foundation and FNDDS archives were supplied by the site owner. Official source references: [USDA FoodData Central downloads](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/download-datasets/), [FoodData Central](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/).

Nutrients use the source's per-100-g edible basis and scale to selected edible mass. Official food portions have their own gram weights. A cup is not a universal mass. Nutrient availability differs by record; an unavailable vitamin or mineral must not be represented as zero. Preserve nutrient names and units, including mg, µg and any source-specific equivalents.

Volume requires a density or recorded volume assumption. Weight force uses standard Earth gravity, 9.80665 m/s²; mass and weight force are different quantities. Density, edible fraction, size, preparation and material can change estimates.

USDA data inclusion does not imply USDA endorsement. Source terms apply independently from this repository's documentation permission.
