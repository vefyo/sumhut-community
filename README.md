# sumhut — Everyday objects and food, in useful numbers.

Estimate mass, weight force, volume and food nutrition with quantities and source-based portions.

[sumhut.com](https://sumhut.com/) · [Community](https://sumhut.com/community)

## What you can do

- Search everyday objects and foods; try “5 apples” or “200 g rice”.
- Adjust counts, grams, kilograms and available container or official food portions.
- Compare mass, volume when density is known, and weight force in newtons.
- Explore calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat and fibre; view reported vitamins and minerals on detailed USDA records.
- Build a combination list, export CSV and share a calculation link.
- Join the community, share photos and videos, or use account-based messaging.
- Add sumhut to your iPhone Home Screen with its own icon.

## Data and accuracy

The October 2026 catalog contains 21,710 records: 20 original references, 8,789 USDA SR28 foods, 469 Foundation foods, 5,432 FNDDS foods and 7,000 non-food estimates. Non-food entries include parameterized material and geometry examples; they are not all measured products. Missing nutrients are unknown, not zero. Reference estimates vary with size, preparation, material and density. Verify labels or measurements when accuracy matters.
