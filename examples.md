# Examples

- [Open the calculator](https://sumhut.com/estimate): search `5 apples`; check the selected apple's mass assumption before using the result.
- [Whole milk](https://sumhut.com/en/foods/whole-milk): choose grams or a recorded official portion, then view the available nutrients. Different milk records and portions can produce different results.
- [Apple](https://sumhut.com/en/foods/apple): change quantity and unit. Share the resulting URL to preserve the calculation state.
- [Combination list](https://sumhut.com/mix): add foods and objects, review totals and export CSV. The browser stores the list; a share link may encode the selected entries. Do not put private information in shared links.
- [Community](https://sumhut.com/community): view discussions; sign in to post or message other users.

Food nutrients and non-food estimates serve different purposes. Non-food entries do not supply food calories. Missing values remain unknown.

Screenshots show the current application running locally with its reference data. They are illustrative, not evidence of an independent measurement.
